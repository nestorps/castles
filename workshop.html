One puzzle at a time: pick a board and how hard it should be · back to the booklet · daily siege

Troops, castles and forests are chosen for the board and the difficulty you ask for, and the line above the sheet says what they came out as. The seed picks among them too, so «Another one» changes the arsenal and the castles, not only where they stand. Not every difficulty fits on every board — a small board has no room to be hard, and the ones it cannot reach are greyed out. Same board, same difficulty and same seed always give the same puzzle, so a sheet can be reprinted. Most come out in a fraction of a second; «Hard» takes the longest, because the search has to throw away most of what it finds to land that high. It gives up after 30 seconds.
