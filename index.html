How to play

Place the whole arsenal on empty cells so that every castle takes exactly the damage in its corner badge — overshooting is as wrong as falling short.

The units

Units only shoot along their row or column, never diagonally. A unit hits every castle in its reach, always for the same damage however far away it is, and range counts to a castle’s nearest cell.

Soldier 1

Strikes a castle on the cell right next to it. Nothing can block it.

Ram 3

Same reach as the soldier — the cell right next to it — but three times the damage. Nothing can block it.

Archer 1

Shoots at a castle 2 or 3 cells away. The shot is flat: a forest, another unit or another castle in between stops it. A castle right next to it is too close.

Catapult 2

Hurls at a castle 3, 4 or 5 cells away. The shot arcs over forests and units; only another castle in between stops it. Anything closer is out of its reach.

The rules

  • The numbers on the right and along the bottom are how many units each row and column holds.
  • No two units touch, not even diagonally. Never on a castle or a forest.
  • Every unit must hit at least one castle.
  • There is exactly one answer, and it is reached without guessing.

Playing

Pick a unit below and tap a cell to place it; tap it again to remove it. Empty pencils in a cell you know stays empty. Hold a cell to clear it. A dashed ring means a unit touches another or reaches nothing.

Your score drops each time you take a unit back off the board or press Check: deduce first, then place. The clock does not count against it.

A new siege every day at midnight.
